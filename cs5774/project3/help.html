What you can do on each page. The Help? button at the bottom of every page brings you straight to that page's section.

All pages

  • Header: the button on the left takes you back up one level (for example, from a textbook to your library), the search box in the middle searches Stacks (try "textbook" or "test"), and Sign out? ends your session.
  • Footer: Help? opens the help section for the page you are on. The sun and moon buttons are placeholders for now.
  • Tooltips: hover over almost anything, or tab to it with the keyboard, to see a short description. Press Escape to dismiss one.
  • Signing in: optional. No page requires it, and being signed in does not change anything yet. The only button that uses it is Sign out?.

Sign In

  • Enter a username and password and press Sign in. Empty fields and wrong details show a message right above the button.
  • Test account (user: admin, password: password) Sign out? on any page brings you back here.

Home

  • My Library opens your library.
  • The search box filters the Recently Visited textbooks as you type. Press Enter or Browse to run a full search on what you typed.
  • Click a recently visited textbook to open its details page.
Go to Home

My Library

  • The ☰ button opens a sidebar over the page with subject filters and other libraries. Click the dimmed area, the ×, or press Escape to close it.
  • The search box filters and ranks the textbooks as you type, matching titles and subjects. Enter or Browse runs a full search on what you typed.
  • Below the list of libraries is a page selector, click the < > to navigate or a number to navigate to a specific page. Searching brings you back to page 1.
  • The person icon opens sharing and permissions, and the + opens the upload page.
  • Click any textbook to see its details. The Edit button next to the title is a placeholder.
Go to My Library

Textbook Details

  • Click the cover or Open to read the textbook. Change Cover lets you pick a new image.
  • Title, author, publisher and description are editable boxes. Drag the bottom corner of a box to make it taller. Press the Save button under a box to keep your change. A “Saved” message confirms it, and the text is still there next time you open this page in the same browser.
  • Subject Tags: click the tag search to open the list, type to filter it, then click a tag, or press Enter to pick the first match. Arrow keys move through the list and Escape closes it.
  • Chosen tags appear under Included. Click the − on a tag to remove it.
  • Delete Book sits under Open. It asks you to confirm in a pop-up. Confirming returns you to your library, and Keep book closes the pop-up.
Go to Textbook Details

Upload Textbook

  • Browse lets you choose a PDF or EPUB file from your computer. (Dragging a file onto the dashed area is not wired up yet. PLACEHOLDER does not work yet)
  • The preview box stands in for the file you picked. Change Cover lets you choose a cover image.
  • Fill in the title, author, publisher and description, and add subject tags the same way as on the details page.
  • Upload and Cancel each ask you to confirm in a pop-up. Confirming either one returns you to your library; Keep editing closes the pop-up. Nothing is actually uploaded yet.
Go to Upload Textbook

Sharing & Permissions

  • The search box filters it by username or initials, and Browse clears the search to show everyone again.
  • People with access have an Edit / View switch. Choose Edit to let them change the library or View to let them only look.
  • Click − to remove someone's access. They move back to the Add state.
  • Click Add next to anyone without access to give them View access.
Go to Sharing & Permissions

Reader

  • The left column lists your annotations, each with Edit and Delete. Add annotation to current page is a placeholder for now.
  • The large area in the middle is the page you are reading. The page number shows at the bottom of the screen shows what page you're on
  • The ☰ button on the right opens reader settings over the page: text size, font choice, bookmarks, a link back to the book details, and a page-number box to jump to a page. Click the dimmed area, the ×, or press Escape to close. (The settings controls are placeholders.)
Go to Reader

Search Results

  • Type in the search box in the header and press Search (or Enter). This is a practice search that only recognises a few words ("textbook", "test").
  • Try "textbook or "test" to see sample results. Any other word shows a friendly "nothing found" message with a link back to your library.
  • The search box on the home and library pages also leads here when you press Enter or Browse.
